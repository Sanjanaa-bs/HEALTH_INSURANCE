# Synthetic Datasets

> [!WARNING]
> The datasets in this directory are **synthetic** and designed exclusively for algorithmic stress-testing, robustness benchmarking, and ablation studies.
> 
> None of the records in `synthetic_hospital_stress_sample.json` should ever be described, claimed, or treated as verified or real Star Health insurer network records. They were synthetically generated/derived from national government facility entries to evaluate entity resolution edge cases across multiple Indian cities.
